Workflow rules

These two switches decide how strictly the Assembly applies its process.

  • Inspection required before issue When off, applications skip the inspection stage unless the service category demands one.
  • Payment before issue When on, a permit cannot be issued until the assessed amount is settled in full.

Stage owners and service standards are configured on the Workflow screen.
